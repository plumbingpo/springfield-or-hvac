Keeping Your Gresham HVAC System Running Longer

Most mid-season breakdowns trace back to skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Swap your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Take a look at the age of your system. Its age tells you a lot. With regular service, most air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is past that point and is also showing higher bills, uneven temperatures, or repeated repairs, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and less stressful than a rushed emergency install in peak season.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A capacitor that's starting to fail is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet that same capacitor, ignored for a season, can take out the compressor and force a full system replacement.
